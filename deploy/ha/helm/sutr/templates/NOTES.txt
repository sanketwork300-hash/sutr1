What this chart does NOT do, so nothing waits for it:

  * It deploys no database. PostgreSQL, and any pooler in front of it, are
    yours to run; point DATABASE_URL at them in the Secret named by
    `existingSecret`.
  * It sets up no cross-region replication. `PLATFORM_MODE=standby` makes a
    region serve reads and refuse writes; promoting one is a database
    operation, not a Helm value.
  * It has not been run against a live cluster by its authors. The manifests
    are rendered and schema-validated in CI; that is not the same thing.
